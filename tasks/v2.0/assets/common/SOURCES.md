# Source scope and rights

The numerical measurement fixtures are original synthetic NRB examples. They are not observations from the papers and do not establish a device mechanism. Device labels in separate experiments are local identifiers.

The archived reading room and figure references were retrieved on 2026-10-17; this release reuses those identified bytes and does not claim a new literature search or new scientific measurements. Exact source records, source scope and article-specific licenses are in source-records.json.

Captions and credits are retained; do not extract precise measurements from these display pixels.
