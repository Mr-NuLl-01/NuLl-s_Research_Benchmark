Research assignment

A methods group needs a 180 mm wide figure for a paper about a short conditioning protocol for a synthetic sensor. The reader needs to see what was done, how individual devices changed, and how the transient measurement varies across devices and batches. Learn a coherent visual approach from the three actual figure references below, then make an original three-panel SVG from the supplied experiment and data.

Experiment record

Twelve independent devices were fabricated in three batches, four devices per batch. Each device was measured once before and once after a 10 minute conditioning step at 25 °C. Values are open-circuit potential in mV. The paired device ID must remain traceable. The separate transient experiment used two independent devices from each batch; at t = 0 s the input was stepped from 0 to 1 in arbitrary units and held through 12 s. Transient outputs are dimensionless normalized responses. Its six device labels are local to this experiment and must not be joined to the paired-potential table. Sampling times are irregular. There is no supplied physical mechanism or statistical significance test.

Deliverable specification

  1. Write a 350–550 word design note that analyses at least one specific, visible design choice from each of P1 Figure 2, P2 Figure 7 and P3 Figure 3. Identify what you adopted, what you changed for these data, and why. General statements such as “use clean colors” are insufficient.
  2. Produce one editable SVG with three coordinated panels: the protocol and measurement relationship; paired before/after data; and the six transient series with an appropriate summary if useful. Preserve data identity and units. Choose scales, offsets, annotations and uncertainty summaries deliberately. Explain the independent unit and batch structure. Do not add a mechanism, p-value, sample or measured curve.
  3. Provide a caption of 160–240 words, define every summary or interval you actually show, and distinguish protocol illustration from data. Show how small absolute changes compare with the operating baseline without disguising the axis.
  4. Export a PNG preview. Check the figure at 180 mm width, aiming for labels at least 7 pt. SVG text must remain live text and panel/data elements separately editable. Include a short evidence-based visual review covering scientific semantics, final-size legibility and export/editability. Package your editable project or regeneration source and a local README in source.zip; the project may use your chosen vector/design tools.
