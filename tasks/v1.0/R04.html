Manuscript assignment

Write a coherent results paragraph and a discussion paragraph for a short paper, using the actual embedded Figure F1, its underlying tables and the experiment record. The intended audience works on measurement methods and should be able to distinguish an observation from a proposed explanation.

Experiment record and rough author notes

The paired and transient datasets describe two distinct synthetic experiments. Conditioning lasted 10 min at 25 °C. Potential readings were recorded to 0.1 mV; calibration uncertainty and long-term drift are not available. No control group, randomization, blinded assessment, mechanistic experiment, failure test or post-12 s observation was supplied. Device is the independent unit for the provided summaries; batch clustering may limit generalization. There is no preregistered hypothesis or supplied inferential test.

Rough notes, to evaluate rather than accept verbatim:
- after treatment potential seems higher, “all devices improve”
- effect is small compared with 1000 mV baseline; need a useful number
- curves go to one by 12 s, perhaps conditioning creates stable transport paths
- three batches show reproducibility, could claim universal stability?
- maybe fit a response time? say clearly what we can resolve
- distinguish mean and spread; connect the figure panels without merging experiments
- next experiment should distinguish mechanism from short-term response

Work

  1. Read both panels at the final size and cross-check their data. Write a 180–260 word Results paragraph that refers explicitly to Figure F1a/F1b and uses at least three relevant, reproducible quantitative statements. Use an analysis appropriate to the sampling and pairing. Do not introduce an inferential result merely because a test is available in a library.
  2. Write a 200–300 word Discussion paragraph that explains the importance of the observations at their actual scale, separates plausible interpretations from demonstrated mechanisms, and proposes one discriminating follow-up measurement with a predicted outcome under two alternatives.
  3. Write a revised 130–190 word figure caption. Define the independent unit, panels, batch identities and interval meaning without claiming measurements absent from the record.
  4. Provide a compact claim-evidence table: claim, exact panel/table location, supporting calculation, status (observation, inference, hypothesis, or unsupported), and scope limit. Include the rough notes you decline to adopt. Supply the small Python calculation script and its output table. Explain any fitted quantity, assumptions and identifiability limits if you choose to fit one.
